**I built and operated an educational content ecosystem from scratch, combining SEO, YouTube, newsletters, and live workshops to introduce Arabic speakers to Personal Knowledge Management through Obsidian.**
## Problem
In early 2024, Arabic educational resources about Personal Knowledge Management and Obsidian were extremely limited. This represented an opportunity to create one of the earliest dedicated Arabic learning platforms around the topic.

## Goal
My goal wasn't to publish isolated pieces of content. It was to design a complete learning journey where every platform served a purpose, from discovery on social media to structured learning through workshops, long-form content, and ongoing education via the newsletter.

## Strategy
I focused entierly on organic growth.

My content engine looked like this:
Twitter -> live classes -> Audience questions -> articles/ videos/ newsltter -> more organic traffic

Every live session acted as audience research.

Instead of guessing what people wanted, I listened to their questions, identified recurring pain points, and turned them into articles, videos, and newsletter content.

That continuous feedback loop made every piece of content more relevant than the previous one.

## Execution
I independently handled every aspect of the project, including content strategy, website development, SEO, newsletter operations, teaching, and community management.
### Research
- Verified there was no direct Arabic competitor.
### Community
- Validated the idea through free workshops.
- Collected feedback.
- Improved the curriculum.
### Website
- Built a WordPress website.
- Created four SEO-focused articles.
- Integrated newsletter and booking system.
### Content
- Produced a comprehensive YouTube crash course.
- Applied insights gathered from dozens of live workshops.

## Results
Within one year, the project evolved from a simple experiment into a small but engaged educational ecosystem driven entirely by organic growth.

- Got the "60 Google Search clicks in 28 days" award on Google Search Console
- 71 newsletter Subscribers
- That YouTube crash course got:
	- +26,000 views
	- +180,000 impressions
	- 8.5% CTR
- Offered 39 live classes
- Published 4 articles
- Recognized by the Obsidian community through a nomination for The Gems of the Year 2024 in the official Obsidian Discord.
## Lessons
- There is strong demand for high-quality Arabic educational content in underserved niches.
- Organic search compounds over time, making early investment worthwhile.
- Direct interaction with learners consistently improved future content quality.
- Audience conversations are often a better source of content ideas than keyword research alone.

## Challenges
- Introducing an unfamiliar concept to an audience that had never heard of PKM.
- Running every part of the project alone.
- Growing entirely through organic reach without paid advertising.
- Explaining technical concepts in simple Arabic.

## What I'd do differently
- Defining a strategy to monetize the project mid-way as it was getting popular
- Document the behind-the-scenes process earlier to build in public and attract other creators interested in PKM
- Publishing an in-depth course about Obsidian and PKM as the mini crash course got many views
